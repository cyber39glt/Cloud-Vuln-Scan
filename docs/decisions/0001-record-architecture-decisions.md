# 0001. Record architecture decisions

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-03

## Context

The project will be open-sourced and maintained over a long time by people who were
not present for early discussions. Security-sensitive choices in particular need
their reasoning preserved.

## Decision

Record each significant architectural or security decision as a short Markdown ADR
in `docs/decisions/`, numbered sequentially, with Context, Decision and Consequences.

## Consequences

- Reviewers and future contributors can see *why*, not just *what*.
- Changing a decision requires writing a new ADR that supersedes the old one.
